Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about onboarding, security compliance, and platform capabilities.

How secure is student data hosted on OSIS? +
OSIS enforces AES-256 bit database encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Our infrastructure conforms strictly to FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) and GDPR guidelines, ensuring student grades and personal details remain confidential.
Can OSIS integrate with our existing Learning Management System (LMS)? +
Yes. OSIS provides bi-directional LTI 1.3 connectors for Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, and Google Classroom to sync rosters, assignments, and grades automatically without double entry.
How long does campus onboarding take? +
Standard institutions complete full migration in 3 to 7 business days. Our automated CSV roster mapping handles student records, faculty rosters, and past grade transcripts smoothly.
Does OSIS work well on mobile phones and tablets? +
Absolutely. Every screen is built with responsive HTML5 and CSS3 layout principles (Flexbox and Grid). Faculty can take roll call from tablets, and students can view GPAs and timetable schedules seamlessly from any smartphone.
